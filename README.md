## Final Year Research Project (Ongoing)

**"From Ribbon to Region: Spatio-Temporal Analysis of Emerging Conurbation along the GT Road Corridor (Lahore–Rawalpindi and Sialkot–Wazirabad), Punjab, Pakistan, 1995–2025"**

A group project (3 students) supervised by Humayun Ashraf, Lecturer. It examines how towns along the GT Road corridor are expanding and merging into a continuous urban region over three decades, using multi-temporal satellite data and spatial analysis. My contribution to the research proposal included the literature review, problem statement, research questions, objectives and work plan.

## Projects

| Project | Data | What I did |
|---|---|---|
| **Surface Water & Turbidity Analysis – Gujranwala District (2025)** | Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized | Mapped annual surface water extent using cloud-masked composites and MNDWI; estimated relative turbidity; analyzed monthly water dynamics and trends (Sen's slope); automated export of statistics and rasters. |
| **Urban Heat Island & Hotspot Analysis – Gujrat District (Summer 2025)** | Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level 2 | Derived land surface temperature, NDVI and NDBI; quantified surface urban heat island intensity against a vegetated rural reference; applied Getis-Ord Gi* hotspot analysis (95% and 99% confidence) to identify significant heat clusters. |
| **Cropping Intensity & Fallow Land Detection – South Punjab (2023–24)** | Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized | Built a 12-month NDVI time series and phenology metrics to classify fallow, single-cropped, double-cropped and built-up land; computed district-level cropping intensity, land utilization and fallow burden indicators with 10 m raster outputs. |
| **Existing Land Use Map – Mansehra City** | Google Earth Engine + ArcGIS Desktop | Combined remote sensing processing in Earth Engine with digitization, parcel editing and cartographic layout in ArcGIS Desktop. |
