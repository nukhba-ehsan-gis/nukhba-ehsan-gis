# Research

## Final Year Research Project (BS Thesis, ongoing)

**Title:** From Ribbon to Region: Spatio-Temporal Analysis of Emerging Conurbation along the GT Road Corridor (Lahore-Rawalpindi and Sialkot-Wazirabad), Punjab, Pakistan, 1995-2025

**Institution:** Department of Geography and GIS, University of Gujrat
**Supervisor:** Humayun Ashraf, Lecturer
**Team:** Group research project (3 students)

### Summary
The study examines how towns along the GT Road corridor are expanding and merging into a continuous urban region over three decades, using multi-temporal satellite data and spatial analysis.
