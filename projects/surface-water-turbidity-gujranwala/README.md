# Surface Water and Turbidity Analysis - Gujranwala District, 2025

**Platform:** Google Earth Engine (JavaScript API)
**Data:** Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized

## Objective
Map annual surface water extent in Gujranwala District and estimate how turbid the water is, then examine how water changes through the year.

## Methods
- Built cloud-masked Sentinel-2 composites.
- Mapped surface water with the Modified Normalized Difference Water Index (MNDWI).
- Estimated relative turbidity within water pixels.
- Analyzed monthly water dynamics and trends using Sen's slope.
- Automated the export of statistics and rasters.

## Outputs
- Annual surface water extent map
- Relative turbidity layer
- Monthly water statistics and trend results
- Exported rasters and tables
