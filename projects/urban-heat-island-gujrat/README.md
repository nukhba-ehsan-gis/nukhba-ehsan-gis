# Urban Heat Island and Hotspot Analysis - Gujrat District, Summer 2025

**Platform:** Google Earth Engine (JavaScript API)
**Data:** Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level 2

## Objective
Quantify surface urban heat island (SUHI) intensity in Gujrat District and identify statistically significant heat clusters.

## Methods
- Derived land surface temperature (LST), NDVI and NDBI.
- Measured SUHI intensity against a vegetated rural reference.
- Applied Getis-Ord Gi* hotspot analysis at 95% and 99% confidence.
- Calculated the share of the district covered by significant heat clusters.

## Outputs
- LST, NDVI and NDBI layers
- SUHI intensity estimate
- Hotspot map (95% and 99% confidence)
