# Cropping Intensity and Fallow Land Detection - South Punjab, 2023-24

**Platform:** Google Earth Engine (JavaScript API)
**Data:** Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized

## Objective
Classify land by cropping pattern and measure cropping intensity and fallow land across South Punjab.

## Methods
- Built a 12-month NDVI time series.
- Derived phenology metrics from the time series.
- Classified land into fallow, single-cropped, double-cropped and built-up classes.
- Computed district-level cropping intensity, land utilization and fallow burden indicators.

## Outputs
- Classified land map at 10 m resolution
- District-level indicator tables
- 10 m raster outputs
